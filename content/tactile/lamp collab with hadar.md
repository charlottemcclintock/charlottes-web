Hadar and I decided to work on some lamps together, and it was so fun to collaborate. We started with ideating and sketching and settled on two tall designs (one round, one squared off) and one short design inspired by these rounded box forms she had been working with.

I threw some of the pieces and wired the lamps, Hadar threw other pieces and assembled the forms.

There was a brief, unsuccessful adventure with wood veneer lampshades - we couldn't make it work for this round, but might try again in the future.
