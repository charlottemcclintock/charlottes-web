outsized joy to effort ratio! was excited to host Christmas in Seattle for the first time for the family. started with cardstock from the art store in four colors and taped every one of these joins while watching a Norweigian disaster show (La Palma). we hung the paper chains from wall hooks using fishing wire. the next morning, a few of the links had opened. a couple days later, it happened again. so I bought a stapler and went back through every join and stapled them. lots of patience. but it really made the apartment feel so joyful and festive!
