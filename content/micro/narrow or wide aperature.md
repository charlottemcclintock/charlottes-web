---
title: narrow or wide aperture
date: 2026-09-24
---

one version of my life is focusing an individually exquisite experience. making art. hanging with friends. putting my feet in the ocean. petting the cat. hugging my mom. curating beautiful spaces that make my brain calm and my heart full (circle wellness, the acorn, hospitality, etc.). chasing a sense of wonder and awe at the earth and the majesty of being alive. being physically strong and fit and capable. making enough money to make this possible.

another version of my life is focusing on the ways I want to shape the world while I’m here. I want a stable climate system. I want everyone to have more economic opportunity and fundamental safety. I want to build the culture we need to [survive technological adolescence](/macro/surviving-technological-adolescence) as a species.
